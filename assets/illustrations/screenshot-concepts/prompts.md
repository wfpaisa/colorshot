# Ilustraciones de funciones

Generadas con la herramienta integrada de imágenes. Sin integración en la interfaz.

## Estilo común

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE standalone landscape 4:3 illustration for a screenshot app, not a collage. Soft 3D-vector hybrid, rounded slightly beveled shapes, smooth gradients, gentle soft drop shadows, very subtle paper grain. Warm off-white clean canvas, cyan coral-orange yellow violet accents with dark navy contrast. Soft studio light top left. Centered single hero object, generous negative space, faint circular halo. Small playful floating rounded pills, white nodes with thin connecting lines, tiny toggles and LEDs, translucent glass parts, sparse sparkles. Clean friendly polished editorial quality. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, photorealism or harsh outlines. 

## Conceptos

- `capture.png`: miniature screenshot camera-machine, navy/cyan display, abstract landscape, coral crop corners, shutter button and captured tile emerging from a slot.
- `annotate.png`: floating screenshot tile, coral stylus, yellow curved annotation arrow and violet translucent highlight.
- `ocr.png`: cyan glass optical scanner on a navy conveyor, coral scan beam, yellow image card transformed into blank violet and white data strips.
- `scroll.png`: screenshot stitching machine, continuous ribbon of abstract webpage panels through cyan rollers, coral downward arrow, navy base.
